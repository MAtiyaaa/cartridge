## Cartridge 0.9.24 · Now Playing

An Android update. Nothing changes on Linux.

### New
- **Cartridge knows what you played on Android.** When you press Play (or Open in PC app), Cartridge notes the game and the time, and the session ends when you come back to Cartridge. Continue playing, Recently played, This week, Most played and your library hours now fill in on Android, and the time is sent to your RomM server as play sessions, as the Linux version does with Steam's play time. It works on its own, with no other app needed. Only games you play from now on count.
- **Games you start from Fuse count too (optional).** With a Fuse version that shares its play sessions, Cartridge reads them when it starts and each time you come back to it, and matches them to your library by RomM id, file or title. Without Fuse nothing changes.

### Changed
- **This week is back on Start on Android**, now that Android has play time.
