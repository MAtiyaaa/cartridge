## Cartridge 0.9.29 · A New Welcome

abdu2304's 0.9.47 to 0.9.52. On Linux, Cartridge works exactly as his 0.9.52 does, with this fork's phone remote and Fuse bridge on top.

### New
- **A new welcome:** the Cartridge mark draws itself and the name rises, then RomM first, your name with your look (Plain or Glass, colour and background, with a picture of each), controls only when your own controller is found, and a progress bar along the top.
- **The Cartridge Animation Engine:** everything that moves follows one set of timings, heavy rather than bouncy, and presses in a row blend instead of snapping. When nothing is pressed for a minute the slow motion pauses, and the background glides to a stop instead of stepping.
- **Find a Setting:** press Y in Settings, or pick Find a Setting, and type what you're looking for.
- **A new colour picker:** a small Cartridge in your colour while you choose, with Hue, Vividness and Brightness sliders for the D-pad, touch and mouse, and suggested colours.
- **Five new backgrounds:** Midnight, Solar Flare, Nordic Aurora, Cyber Gradient and Liquid Titanium.
- **Performance Overlay** (Settings → Help and About): frame rate, slowest frame and memory in a corner of the screen.
- **A soft rumble when things settle:** a tile dropping into place on Start, a cover landing on its page, the end of a list.
- **Trophies and Gamerscore from every device,** including games not installed anywhere else.
- **Linux (from abdu2304):** Cartridge Save Sync (your saves on your own RomM, checked before every game and saved after it, held on the device while RomM can't be reached), mods from GameBanana, EmuCoreX and Nexus Mods with a rule book per emulator, ROM hacks (Beta), every cover kept small for browsing away from the server, the Game Mode screen on the first desktop launch, a new Quick Menu, Vita3K checked to really start, Always Ask where downloads go.

### Changed
- **Settings, sorted:** Library, Emulators, Achievements, Look & Feel, Controls, Downloads and Updates, Help and About (plus Phone Remote, and Android on Android), each with a line saying what's in it.
- **Achievements on one page:** your latest unlocks and every game you've played, in a grid or a stack, sorted the way you like.
- **Glass bends what's behind it** like a lens on the Dock, search, pop-ups and toasts, and steps down to plain frosted glass if frames get slow.
- **The hello slides out of the Dock,** letter by letter.
- **Aurora, Contours, Drift and Tide** are rebuilt, and GIF search for Start's picture widget asks Tenor first.
- **Unnamed PS4 games get their names everywhere** once one of your devices knows them.

### Fixed
- **The background could make people feel sick** after a while: game art behind pages no longer zooms, and idle no longer judders.
- **Focus you couldn't see** in Light, OLED and Glass, and focused achievement cards with dark text on a dark card.
- **Glass:** sharp strips along sheets and the Dock, and Light Glass buttons that looked black.
- **The tour's A** stopped working after Continue went to another page; its search step now counts on every page.
- **Tapping Settings' list** now highlights the section, as the controller does.
- **Home** no longer jumps or flickers when coming back from a game, and **Start's** page overview no longer shows stray bars.
- **Trophy notes failing with error 500** in RomM.
- **The phone pairing overlay** follows Plain and Glass: blurred only in Glass.
- **Linux (from abdu2304):** Vita3K that didn't open on SteamOS, PS3 firmware installs reported as failed, new games going to the SD card when you picked This Device.
